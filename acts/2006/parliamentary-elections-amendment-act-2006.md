## Section 1

Amendment of section 8 of the Parliamentary Elections Act 2005 Section 8 of the Parliamentary Elections Act 2005 is amended in subsection (3) by substituting for the words “supported by not less than two thirds of all members of Parliament”, the words— “supported by a simple majority of members of Parliament’.
