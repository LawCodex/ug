# The Appropriation Act, 2007

ACTS SUPPLEMENT No. 5 28th September, 2007. ACTS SUPPLEMENT to The Uganda Gazette No. 54 Volume C dated 28th September, 2007. Printed by UPPC, Entebbe, by Order of the Government. Act 9 Appropriation Act 2007 THE APPROPRIATION ACT, 2007. An Act to apply a sum of four thousand six hundred and twenty billion, sixty five million, two hundred nineteen thousand shillings out of the Consolidated Fund to the service of the year ending on the 30th June, 2008 and to appropriate the supplies granted. DATE OF ASSENT: 25th September, 2007. Date of Commencement: Ist July, 2007. BE IT ENACTED by Parliament as follows: 1. Commencement. This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the Ist day of July, 2007. 2. Issue of money out of the Consolidated Fund. The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted to the Government for the service of the year ending on the 30th June, 2008, the sum of four thousand six hundred and twenty billion, sixty five million, two hundred nineteen thousand shillings. 1 = NE ———_————————EeEEEE——_———==
