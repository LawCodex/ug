| An Act to amend the Uganda Revenue Authority Act to modify the membership of the Board of Directors of Uganda Revenue Authority; to bar the suing of the Commissioner General in his or her official or personal capacity; to provide for appointment by the board of directors of Uganda Revenue Authority officers from

the level of assistant commissioner or deputy head of department or higher; and to provide for other related purposes.
