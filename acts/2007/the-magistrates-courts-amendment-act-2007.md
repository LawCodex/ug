Act Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment) Act 2007 THE MAGISTRATES’ COURTS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2007. An Act to amend the Magistrates’ Courts Act. DATE OF ASSENT: Date of Commencement: BE IT ENACTED by Parliament as follows:

## Section 1

Abolition of Grade III Magistrate The post of Magistrate Grade III is abolished and accordingly, section 4 of the Magistrates’ Courts Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act is amended in subsection (2) by the repeal of paragraph (d).

## Section 9

Amendment of section 182 of the principal Act Section 182 of the principal Act is amended in subsection (4), by the repeal of the words “other than a Magistrate Grade IIT’.

## Section 10

Amendment of section 204 of the principal Act Section 204 of the principal Act is amended as follows—

## Section 11

Amendment of section 207 of the principal Act Section 207 of the principal Act is amended in subsection (1) as follows—

(a) in paragraph (a), by substituting for “five million shillings” the words “fifty million shillings”;

(b) in paragraph (b) by substituting for “two million shillings” the words “twenty million shillings”;

(c) by repealing paragraph (d).

## Section 12

Repeal of Schedule 2 to the principal Act Schedule 2 of the principal Act is repealed.

## Section 13

Disapplication of Schedules 1 and 3 of the principal Act to Magistrate Grade III Schedules 1 and 3 to the principal Act shall cease to apply to Magistrate Grade III and therefore any reference in those Schedules to Magistrate Grade III is repealed.
