### Section 8

Access to vaccines. The State shall ensure that every Ugandan required to be immunised under this Act or any other related law, has access to vaccines.

### Section 9

Penalties.

(l) Subject to sections 7 and 8, a person, who without lawful excuse contravenes sections 3 (l), 5 or 6 commits an offence and is liable, on conviction, to a fine not exceeding twelve currency points or imprisonment not exceeding six months or to both.

### Section 10

Administration of vaccines in extraordinary cases.

(l) The Minister shall, by statutory instrument, order the

administration of vaccines in the following extraordinary cases-

(a) where a person has not been vaccinated in accordance with the Second Schedule to this Act;

(b) in case of an epidemic:

(c) when there is danger of entry of transmissible diseases into the country;

(d) upon detcction of a new infectious agent or an infectious atent deemed controlled or eradicated has re-appeared; or

(e) whenever so required, pursuant to applicable international practices.
