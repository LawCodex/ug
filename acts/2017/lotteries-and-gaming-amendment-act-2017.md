Act Lotteries and Gaming (Amendment) Act 2017 THE LOTTERIES AND GAMING (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2017 An Act to amend the Lotteries and Gaming Act, 2016 to reduce the rate of gaming tax from thirty five percent to twenty percent. DATE OF ASSENT: Date of Commencement: Be it enacted by Parliament as follows:

## Section 1

Commencement. This Act shall come into force on Ist July, 2017.

## Section 2

Amendment of Schedule 4 of the Lotteries and Gaming Act, 2016, Act 7 of 2016. The Lotteries and Gaming Act, 2016, is amended in Schedule 4 by substituting for “thirty five percent” the words “twenty percent”.
