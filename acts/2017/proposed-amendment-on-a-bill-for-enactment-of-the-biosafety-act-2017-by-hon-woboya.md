"strict liability" under this act means liability for adverse effects caused deliberately or directly as a result of genetic engineering research, manufacture, processing, transit, export or import provided cause and suffering are duly proven.

Justification To provide clarity regarding the scope of strict liability which has been grossly misunderstood by commentators on GEM tech nology.

Proposed amendment to clause 35.

lnsert the statement "based on the circumstances of the case, be subject to strict or fault-based liability" between the word shall and damage...in Clause 35

Amended Clause 35 to read:

"A proprietor or an individual developer of genetically engineered material under this Act shall, based on the circumstances of the case, be subject to strict

or fault-based liability for any damage, harm, inconvenience or loss caused to the community livelihood, indigenous knowledge system or technologies, environment, biodiversity, ecosystem, species of flora and fauna or human and animal health".

Justification: o

To provide for both strict and fault-based liability for biosafety claims and

applicable liability based on the circumstances of the claim Strict liability provisions alone are likely to disenfranchise our farmers who may be sued for faults that are not their own.
