THE REPUBLIC OF UGAIWA

THE UGANDA COMMUNICATiONS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2017 An Act to amend section 93 of the Uganda Communications Act, 2013 to remove the requirement for Parliamentary approval of regulations made by the Minister under the Act. DATE OF ASSUNT: Date of Commencement: BE IT ENACTED by Parliament as follows:

## Section 1

Amendment of Act No. 1 of 2013. Section 93 of the Uganda Communications Act, 2013 is amendedin subsection (1) by repealing the words "and with the approval of Parliament"; by inserting a new subsection immediately after subsection 3 as follows"(3a) Parliament may by resolution revoke the regulations or a particular provision of the regulations within 30 days from the date the regulations are laid"
