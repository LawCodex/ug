Act Stamp Duty (Amendment) Act 2016 THE STAMP DUTY (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2016 An Act to amend the Stamp Duty Act, 2014, to vary the duty chargeable on certain instruments under the Act. DATE OF ASSENT: Date of Commencement: BE IT ENACTED by Parliament as follows:

## Section 1

Commencement. This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on Ist July, 2016.

## Section 2

Amendment of Stamp Duty Act, 2014. The Stamp Duty Act, 2014 is amended in Schedule 2—

(a) by substituting for the stamp duty of Shs.5,000 wherever it appears, Shs. 10,000;

(b) in item 31, EXCHANGE OF PROPERTYof the total value, by substituting for the stamp duty rate of 1%, a rate of 2%;

(c) in item 62(a), Transfer - of the total value , by substituting for the stamp duty rate of 1%, a rate of 1.5%; and

(d) in item 64, by substituting for the word “provision” the word “instrument.”
