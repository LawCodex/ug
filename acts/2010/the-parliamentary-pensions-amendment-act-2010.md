THE PARLIAMENTARY PENSIONS ACT, 2010.

(AMENDMENT)

An Act to amend the Parliamentary Pensions Act to make provision for the type of pension scheme; a vesting scale stipulating the proportion of Government contribution that shall vest on the member upon retirement; a disability pension; fair representation of members on the board of trustees; a dispute resolution mechanism; and for other related matters.
