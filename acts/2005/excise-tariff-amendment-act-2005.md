>| THE EXCISE TARIFF (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2005. An Act to amend Schedule 2 of the Excise Tariff Act, Cap 338, to provide for new rates of excise duty to be charged on specified goods and to specify other goods on which excise duty is to be imposed. DATE OF ASSENT: Date of Commencement: BE IT ENACTED by Parliament as follows—

## Section 1

Short title and commencement

(1) This Act may be cited as the Excise Tariff (Amendment) Act, 2005.

(2) This Act shall come into force on Ist July 2005, except for item 7 of Schedule 2 which shall come into force on 8th June, 2005.

## Section 2

Amendment of Schedule 2 The Excise Tariff Act is amended by substituting for Schedule 2 the following—
