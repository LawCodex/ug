# Public Holiday Act (CAP.255) 2005

LEGAL NOTICES SUPPLEMENT No. 9 27th July, 2005. LEGAL NOTICES SUPPLEMENT .to The Uganda Gazette No. 45 Volume XCVIII dated 27th July, 2005. Printed by UPPC, Entebbe, by Order of the Government. Legal Notice No. 11 of 2005. THE PUBLIC HOLIDAYS ACT (CAP. 255). Declaration of a Public Holiday. (Under section 2(2) of the Public Holidays Act, Cap. 255) IN EXERCISE of the powers conferred upon me by section 2(2) of the Public Holidays Act, I declare the 28th day of July, cap. 255. 2005 to be observed as a public holiday throughout Uganda for purposes of enabling citizens to vote in the referendum, under article 74(1)(a) of the Constitution.
