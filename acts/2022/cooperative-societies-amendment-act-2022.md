An Act to amend the Cooperative Societies Act to define beneficial owners for the purposes of cooperative societies; to require cooperative societies to keep a register of beneficial ownersl to empower the Minister to make regulations for generally giving effect to the provisions on beneficial ownership and for related matters. Dars on AssrNr: Date of Commencement:

BE IT ENACTED by Parliament as follows-

## Section 1

Amendment of section I of Cooperative Societies Act Section I of the Cooperative Societies Act, in this Act referred to as the "principal Act", is amended by inserting in the appropriate place the following definition"beneficial owner" means a natural person who ultimately owns or controls a cooperative society or the natural person on whose behalfa transaction is conducted in the cooperative society, and includes a natural person who exercises ultimate control over a cooperative society;".

The principal Act is amended by inserting immediately after section 12 the following-

"12A^. Register of beneficial owners

(1) A cooperative society with beneficial owners shall keep a register of its beneficial owners and shall enter in the register the following particulars-

(a) the names, contact and postal addresses of each beneficial owner;

(b) the national identification numbers (NIN) of the beneficial owners;

(") the nature ofthe beneficial ownership;

(d) the date on which each beneficial owner was entered in the register as a beneficial owner;

(")

the date on which any person ceased to be a beneficial owner; and

(f)

any other information as the Minister may prescribe by regulations.

(2) The register ofbeneficial owners shall be kept at the registered office of the cooperative society except that-

(a) if the work of making it up is done at another office of the cooperative society, it may be kept at that other office; and

(b) if the cooperative society arranges with some other person for the making up of a register on behalf of the cooperative society by that other person, it may be kept at the office of that person at which the work is done but it shall not be kept at a place outside Uganda.
