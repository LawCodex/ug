## Section 1

Commencement. This Act shall come into force on the l" day of July, 2022.

## Section 2

Issue of money out of the Consolidated Fund. The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted to the Government for the service of the year ending on the 30'h June, 2023 the sum of thirty trillion, two hundred eighty seven billion, seven hundred sixty seven million, and nine hundred thirteen thousand shillings only.

## Section 3

Appropriation. The sums granted by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes and in the amounts specified in columns I and 2 of the Schedule to this Act.

## Section 4

Expiry of appropriations.

(1) Any part of the sums granted by section 3 of this Act which has been, or is issued from the Consolidated Fund on or before the 306 day ofJune,2023 shall expire and cease to have any effect at the close

of the financial year.
