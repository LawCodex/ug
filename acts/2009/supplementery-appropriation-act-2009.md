Supplementary appropriation. The supplementary appropriation out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the first, second, third and fourth quarters of the financial year 2008/2009, for the purposes specified in column |! of the Schedule to this Act, of the sums respectively specified in column 2 of that Schedule, is hereby allowed and confirmed. au
