*(2) Probate or letters ofadministration granted by a court of a country other than Uganda shall only be resealed in a court in Uganda where the relevant law under which the letters of administration or probate was granted is not contrary to the Succession Act, Cap. 162, the Administration of Estates (Small Estates) (Special Provisions) Act, Cap. 156 or the Estate of Missing Persons (Management) Act, Cap. 159.
