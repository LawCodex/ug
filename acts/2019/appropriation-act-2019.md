THE APPROPRIATION ACT, 2019. An Act to apply a sum of twenty-seven trillion, nine hundred lifty seyen billion, one hundred sixty three million, four hundred nineteen thousand shillings only out of the Consolidated Fund and to apply a sum of four hundred forty-five billion, eight hundred nineteen million, one hundred thousand shillings out of the Petroleum Fund to the service of the year ending on the 30th June,2020 and to appropriate the supplies granted. Dere or Assrvr: Date of commencement: Be n rNecreo by Parliament as follows:

1.

Commencement. This Act shall come into force on the lst day of July, 2019.

2\. Issue of money out of the Consolidated Fund.

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted to the Government for the Service of the year ending on the 30th June, 2020 the sum of twenty seven trillion, nine hundred fifty seven billion, one hundred sixty three million, four hundred nineteen thousand shillings only.

3.

Issue of money out of the Petroleum Fund. The Treasury may issue out of the Petroleum Fund and apply towards the supply granted to the Government for the service of the year ending on the 30th June, 2020 the sum of four hundred fourty-five billion, eight hundred nineteen million, one hundred thousand shillings only.

4\. Appropriation. The sums granted by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes and in the amounts specified in columns I and2 of the Schedule to this Act.

5\. Expiry of appropriations.

2019

(l) Any part of the sums granted by sections 2 and 3 of this Act which has been, or is issued from the Consolidated Fund or the Peffoleum Fund on or before the 30th day of June, 2020 shall expire and cease to have any effect at the close of the financial year for which it is made.

(2) A vote that does not expend money that was appropriated to the vote for the financial year shall at the close of the financial year, repay the money to the Consolidated Fund or the Petroleum Fund, as the case may be.
