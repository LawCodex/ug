## Section 3

Objectives

(1) The main objective of this Act is to give effect to the Government Policy for Rationalisation of Govemment Agencies and Public Expenditure adopted by the Cabinet on 22'd February, 2021 and contained in Cabinet Minute No. 43(CT 2021).

(2) Without prejudice to the general effect of subsection (1), the specific objective of this Act is

(a) to mainstream the mandate and functions of the Agricultural Chemicals Board into the Ministry;

(b) to eliminate the bloated structure and functional ambiguities of the Agricultural Chemicals Board: and

(c) to achieve effective and eflicient service delivery.
