# The appropriation act 2008

ACTS SUPPLEMENT No. 10 PARLIAMENT LAR RARE, 008. ACTS SUPPLEMER?* 717°. to The Uganda Gazette No. 52 Vplume CI dated [71 bax, 8. Printed by UPPC, Entebbe: by Order eS: Will = Act 17 Appropriation Act 2008 THE APPROPRIATION ACT, 2008. An Act to apply a sum of five thousand seven hundred and thirty eight billion, four hundred and two million, seventy five thousand shillings out of the Consolidated Fund to the service of the year ending on the 30th June, 2009 and to appropriate the supplies granted. DATE OF ASSENT: 4th October, 2008. Date of Commencement: Ist July, 2008. BE IT ENACTED by Parliament as follows:

## Section 1

Commencement. This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the Ist day of July, 2008.

## Section 2

Issue of money out of the Consolidated Fund. The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted to the Government for the service of the year ending on the 30th June, 2009, the sum of five thousand seven hundred and thirty eight billion, four hundred and two million, seventy five thousand shillings. ee

## Section 3

Appropriation. The sums granted by this Act shall be appropriated for the services | and purposes and in the amounts specified in columns 1 and 2 of the Schedule to this Act. | 4, Extension of duration of appropriation. | Any part of the sums granted by section 2 of this Act which has been, or | is issued from the Consolidated Fund on or before the 30th day of June, 2009 shall be available until a date determined by the Secretary to the Treasury (not being later than the 31st day of October, 2009) for making payments which become due during the year ending on the 30th day of June, 2009 to an accounting officer or a receiver of revenue or to an officer administering a fund established under section 9 of the Public Finance and | Accountability Act, 2003. |
